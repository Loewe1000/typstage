# Where these images come from

The six plates in `mosaic-editorial.typ` stand in for the photographs of the
*Cream, Green, and Black Geometric Blocks Clean Minimal Presentation* template
by SlidesCarnival, which this deck is modelled on. **None of the template's
photographs are used here**, and none of them are redistributed with this
package.

The template's layouts are CC BY 4.0 and are credited in the deck header. Its
photographs are not credited per file, so they cannot be carried over
responsibly.

## What these files are

Synthetic images, generated on **29 August 2026** with OpenAI's `gpt-image-2`
through the OpenAI Images API at quality `low`. Nothing was traced, sampled or
reproduced from an existing photograph. Each was generated from the prompt
below as a PNG, resized with Lanczos so the long edge is 820 px, and saved as
WebP at quality 74.

They keep to the register the template works in, read off its own pictures:
warm minimal interiors and still lifes, ceramics, dried grasses, wood and
marble, and one dark botanical texture. Colour is kept — unlike the greyscale
deck next door, this one's argument is a warm palette, not a single ink.

No people appear in any of them. The template has two portraits; a synthetic
portrait would put a face that belongs to nobody in front of a room, and the
deck can make its point without one. On the team slide the plates therefore
carry still lifes where the template carried portraits.
